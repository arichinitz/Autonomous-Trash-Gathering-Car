# Autonomous-Trash-Gathering-Car
 CIJE Hackathon 2024 1st Place - Car that detects and gathers green trash bins

## The challenge
- Build an autonomous car that can gather mini green trash bins by itself

## Features
- Self-driving
- Garbage-locating
- Garbage-gathering

## Tools Used
- Arduino IDE
- HuskyLens

## How to Use
Plug in the battery... the car will do the rest  

## How it works
- Bricks are moved from the bin to the chamber on a custom conveyor belt
- Bricks are fed one by one into the chamber, where color is determined
- Chamber orients to one of three directions (one of three colors), and solenoid (which is holding the brick in place) releases the brick
- Brick falls into its respective color track, falling down its size hole

## Results
CIJE Hackathon 2024 - Gathered the most garbage bins and won 1st place
